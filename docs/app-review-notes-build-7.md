# App Review Notes for The Rise 1.0 (7)

This build addresses the July 24, 2026 App Review rejection for auto-renewable subscription metadata and subscription review readiness.

The in-app The Rise Pro subscription screen includes:

- Subscription title: The Rise Pro
- Monthly subscription: The Rise Pro Monthly, 1 month, $6.99 per month
- Annual subscription: The Rise Pro Annual, 1 year, $49.99 per year
- Auto-renewal language explaining renewal and cancellation timing
- Restore Purchases button
- Functional Privacy Policy link: https://mattbrown0406.github.io/TheRise/privacy.html
- Functional Terms of Use link using Apple Standard EULA: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
- Functional Support link: https://mattbrown0406.github.io/TheRise/support.html

Reviewer path:

1. Launch The Rise. No account is required.
2. Tap Pro in the bottom navigation.
3. Use the Monthly / Annual toggle to review either plan.
4. Tap Subscribe Monthly or Subscribe Annual to open Apple's purchase confirmation.
5. Tap Restore Purchases to verify restore access.

The app uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

Please also verify that both auto-renewable subscription products are selected in the app version's In-App Purchases and Subscriptions section in App Store Connect:

- therise_pro_monthly
- therise_pro_annual
