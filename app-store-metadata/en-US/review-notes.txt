AUTO-RENEWABLE SUBSCRIPTIONS
To locate the subscriptions:
1. Launch The Rise. No account is required.
2. Tap Pro in the bottom navigation.
3. The Pro screen displays The Rise Pro Annual by default.
4. Use the Monthly / Annual toggle to display The Rise Pro Monthly or The Rise Pro Annual.
5. Tap Subscribe Monthly or Subscribe Annual to open Apple’s purchase confirmation.

Before purchase, the Pro screen displays the selected subscription title, duration, localized StoreKit price, auto-renewal and cancellation disclosure, Restore Purchases, Privacy Policy, and Terms of Use.

WHAT THE SUBSCRIPTION UNLOCKS
The Rise Pro unlocks four features inside the app. Each is visible in a locked state before purchase and becomes usable immediately after:
1. Flow history - Waters > select a gauged river (Lower Deschutes, Middle Deschutes, Upper Deschutes, Metolius, Crooked River, Fall River) > Flow history. Charts the USGS gauge readings this device has recorded.
2. Rating breakdown - Today > Why this rating. The full numeric explanation behind a water's 1-10 score.
3. Fish this plan - Waters > select any water > Fish This Plan. A staged plan for the current window.
4. Hatch ID assistant - Bugs > Hatch ID Assistant. Filters by size, colour, water type and behaviour.

The app sends no notifications and requires no account. Water rankings, live USGS and NWS readings, hatches, fly recommendations, the bug library, and the catch log are free.

PHOTO PICKER
To verify the catch-photo flow:
1. Tap Log.
2. Tap New Entry.
3. Tap Choose File in the Photo field.
4. Choose Camera or Photo Library and grant the requested permission.
5. Select a photo, complete the required catch fields, and save the entry.

Build 10 includes NSCameraUsageDescription, NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription, and NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription.
